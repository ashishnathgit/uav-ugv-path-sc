Dataset Limitations in the Original Paper & Our Engineering Workarounds dataset.py & models.py
1. Limitation: Heavy External GeoTIFF Dependency (LandCover.ai [28] > 2.5 GB)

The paper uses 41 ultra-high-resolution aerial orthophotos (9000×9500 pixels) of rural Poland, requiring multi-gigabyte external downloads and an RTX A6000 (48 GB VRAM) to train a 12-layer ViT.
Workaround Implemented: AerialPathPlanningDataset in dataset.py includes a hybrid drop-in loader for real LandCover.ai GeoTIFFs/PNGs plus a Multi-Biome Procedural Aerial Orthophoto Generator (generate_aerial_scene()) using fractal noise, winding river basins, road networks, and shadowed building footprints.

2. Limitation: Severe Rural Bias (36 Rural vs. only 5 Urban Maps in LandCover.ai)

As shown in Section VI-B (Fig. 6), 88% of LandCover.ai is rural/forest with homogeneous areas that are easy to interpolate, whereas dense urban maps cause high path-planning errors.
Workaround Implemented: Our generator explicitly exposes biome="urban" vs. biome="rural" (adjustable right in the top toolbar!) to synthesize dense urban building blocks and road intersections on demand.

3. Limitation: Missing Explicit Traversability Ground-Truth Labels in Raw RGB

Raw aerial images lack ground-truth UGV traversability weights τ_u ∈ [0, 1], and pure MSE pixel loss wastes bits on tree canopy textures rather than obstacle contours.
Workaround Implemented: We generate paired (RGB, semantic_mask, tau_gt) tuples, implement the Borges et al. [24] soft prototype classifier rgb_to_traversability(), and inject a spatial boundary salience prior into SemComTransceiver._compute_patch_semantic_prior().

Core Mathematical Formulation Implemented in Code

Eq. (1): Traversability Graph Edge Weight
w(u, v) = (κ / τ_u)² + (κ / τ_v)²

Maps vertex traversability τ_u ∈ (0, 1] to 8-connected graph edge weights for Dijkstra shortest path planning.

Proposition 1 & Eq. (15): Path Sensitivity ξ
ξ = f(z) = |z| · φ(z) = (|z| / √2π) exp(-z²/2)

Identifies candidate paths most sensitive to variance reduction, peaking at |z| = Δw_j / √(σ_{p_t}² + σ_{p_j}²) = 1 (Eq. 16).

Eqs. (17)–(22): Windowed MC-LBC & UAV δ_k
ψ̃_{u,h}(t) = ψ_{u,h}^{off} + Δψ_{u,h}^{on}(t), δ_k = 1 / (1 + e^{-g(Ψ̃_k - Ψ_h)})

Combines offline LBC precomputation with online Monte Carlo path sampling inside corridor window W_t = N_r(C_t ∪ F_t).
